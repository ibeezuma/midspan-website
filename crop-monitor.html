The municipality, fields, crops and values are invented for demonstration, including the season. The index is NDVI-style greenness from 0 to 1 and is not taken from any satellite. A real analysis would use cloud-masked imagery from Sentinel-2 or similar, plus field boundaries and seeding records from the producers.

Method: each field has a weekly value. A field is flagged when it sits 0.10 or more below the median of all fields growing the same crop in the same week. A flagged field with a flagged neighbour on any side or corner suggests a shared cause such as hail, dry soil or a wet spring. An isolated flagged field is more likely a management or disease issue. Both are prompts to look at the field, not diagnoses.
