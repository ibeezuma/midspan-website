Every elevation model, volume and stockpile on this page is synthetic. The site, schedule and delays are invented for demonstration and do not describe real work.

Method: each flight is differenced against the baseline on a 1.5 m grid. Changes under 10 cm are ignored. Cut is lowered ground, fill is raised ground. Stockpile areas are excluded from cut and fill and measured above bare ground. Remaining to grade is the absolute difference to the design surface, and progress is one minus remaining divided by the baseline remaining.
