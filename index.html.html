drone and GIS

Most drone mapping engagements involve two separate vendors: a drone operator who captures data and a GIS team that processes it. The handoff between them introduces delays, interpretation errors, and coordinate system mismatches that erode the value of the original survey.

What the certifications
actually mean

GISP
Geographic Information Systems Professional
GIS Certification Institute (GISCI)

The GISP is the only internationally recognized, vendor-neutral professional credential in GIS. Held by fewer than 30,000 practitioners worldwide, it validates education, professional experience, and contributions to the profession. Government and utility clients increasingly require or score the GISP in formal procurement evaluation criteria.

International standardVendor-neutralProcurement-scored
UN
ArcGIS Utility Network Associate
Esri Technical Certification

ArcGIS Utility Network is Esri's current-generation utility data model, replacing the legacy Geometric Network across the industry. This certification validates hands-on knowledge of domain networks, structure networks, subnetwork configuration, trace networks, and connectivity rules — the core components of every UN implementation. One of the most technically demanding Esri certifications available.
