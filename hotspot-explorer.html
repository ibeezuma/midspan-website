How far does clustering reach?

Moran's I by neighbourhood distance. It falls as the distance grows past the size of the patches.

The reserve, plots, cover values and place names are invented for demonstration, and nothing here describes a real place or survey.

Method: Getis-Ord Gi* with binary weights (every plot within the chosen distance, itself included) and a normal approximation for p-values. Global Moran's I uses the same neighbours without the plot itself, with a pseudo p-value from 999 random reshuffles. Bonferroni multiplies each p-value by the number of plots. Benjamini-Hochberg controls the share of false alarms among the plots flagged. Real analyses should also check edge effects and spatial sampling bias: here, plots follow trails and roads more densely than open ground, so patterns near trails carry more weight.
