Overall ranking

Score out of 100. The line marks 70, the level this demo treats as ready enough.

Every community, number and route here is invented for demonstration. They do not describe any real place or people, and any resemblance of a name to a real place is coincidence.

How scores work: each area is scored 0 to 100, higher is better. Water uses plant condition, certified operator and advisory status (an advisory of 12 months or more takes a further 10 points off). Access uses route type and condition. Power uses grid or diesel supply and outage hours. Broadband uses the best service tier. Housing uses major repairs and crowding. Overall is water 30%, access 20%, power 20%, broadband 15%, housing 15%. This is an illustrative index, not an official measure.

In a real project the community owns its data and decides who sees it and how it is used. A tool like this would run on the community's own system under its own access rules.
