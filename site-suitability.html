What drives the top scores

Weighted points by criterion for the five best sites.

The study area, terrain, river, wetlands, homes, roads, transmission lines and land ownership are all invented for demonstration. Nothing here describes a real place, and a real screening would use surveyed data and an engineer's judgement.

How scoring works: each criterion is turned into a 0 to 100 score on a 200 m grid. Grid connection falls to 0 at 8 km from the nearest line. Load proximity falls to 0 at 12 km from the industrial park. Road access is full marks within 300 m and falls to 0 at 5 km. Terrain is full marks up to 2% slope and 0 at 15%. Flood and wetlands is 0 inside the floodplain or a wetland and full marks 1 km clear. Community separation is 0 at 300 m from a home and full marks at 1.5 km. Land scores 100 for public, 45 for private and 0 for protected. The site score is the weighted average.
