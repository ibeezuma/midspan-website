The hamlet, lots, zones, rules, distances and bylaw numbers are invented for demonstration and are not real regulations. A real screening tool would load the municipality's land use bylaw, surveyed lot lines, AER pipeline and well data, and flood hazard mapping, and its output would still be reviewed by a development officer.

Method: distances are measured from the nearest edge of the footprint to the feature in metres. Setbacks are measured from the lot lines, front on the street side. The floodplain rule fails if any part of the footprint touches the floodplain. The finder tries positions every 1 m inside the lot and picks the compliant one closest to where the building is now.
