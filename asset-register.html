The county, its assets, costs, ages and conditions are invented for demonstration. Costs and expected lives are illustrative and are not engineering estimates, and a real asset management plan would use inspection data and the municipality's own unit costs.

Method: condition is the share of expected life used (age divided by expected life, with inspection noise). Very good is under 30%, good under 55%, fair under 80%, poor under 100%, and very poor at or past the end of expected life. In each forecast year every asset uses another 1 divided by its expected life. Assets at 100% are due. Funded assets are replaced and restart at zero. The long-run need is the average yearly replacement cost over 30 years if every asset were replaced the year it came due.
